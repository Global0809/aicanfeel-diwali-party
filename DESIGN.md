## Composition and motion

A sparse fixed brand/date header has no menu. Three open stage panels carry witty copy and facts. The camera follows DIWALI_CAMERA.progress0→1 only: distance2.1→15.5units and angle-16°→47°. No backward zoom, pointer camera drift or independent entry tween. ScrollTrigger scrub is0.65s; native damping is frame-time based.

Gold ember trails, native neon bars, a short glass veil and clipped type entrances support the main camera movement. The ending changes from one Yayyyy button into a landscape foil pass on desktop and a portrait pass on phones. Finite fireworks precede gentle hover/tilt. Reduced motion removes decorative movement and fireworks.
